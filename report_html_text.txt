4. Scope Boundaries & Limitations
This study benchmarks distribution-free risk guarantees on surgical/ablation candidates from KiTS23. All patients have kidney tumours; hence patient-level specificity (false-alarm rate in healthy cohorts) cannot be estimated. Guarantees hold conditionally under exchangeability with the training and calibration distributions.
